Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026 · wraptvending.com

Who we are

Wrapt places, stocks, and services smart vending machines for businesses in the Franklin, Spring Hill, and Greater Nashville, Tennessee area. This policy covers what we collect through wraptvending.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request a proposal or refer a location, we collect what you type into the form: your name, business, role, contact details, venue information, and anything you add in the notes. We also keep simple, anonymous visit counts (for example, how many people arrived from one of our cards versus a search engine). We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use it

We use what you submit for one purpose: responding to your request — building your proposal, answering your question, or scouting the location you referred. Your contact details stay with us and the service providers that make the site run (form handling, text messaging), who process them only on our behalf.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number and ask us to call or text, we may text you about your request. Message frequency varies with the conversation; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of texts, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of doing business with Wrapt.

Keeping and deleting

We keep lead information for as long as we're working with you or your location. Want your information removed? Email paige@wraptvending.com or text 615.948.2976 and we'll delete it.
