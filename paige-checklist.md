## THE MOMENT THINGS HAPPEN

### When the first machine is scheduled
- [ ] Photos at install: the wrapped machine straight-on, in its space,
      and a close-up of the wrap. Send to Lord → they go on the website
      the same day
- [ ] Upload the same photos to the Google Business Profile

### Within a week of the first install
- [ ] Ask the host, in person: "Would you mind leaving us a quick
      Google review?" — then text them the review link so it's one tap.
      The first 5 reviews matter more than the next 50.
- [ ] Ask one sentence for the website: how'd it go, what do their
      people think? That quote + their name + business = the site's
      first testimonial

### When someone refers a location
- [ ] Thank the referrer and keep them posted — referrals are goodwill,
      not paid (no payout is promised anywhere)

## YOUR EVERYDAY RHYTHM (once live)
- Morning: digest text arrives at your phone — new leads, hot ones
  flagged, anyone going stale
- New lead text arrives → reply or call within the hour when you can;
  speed is the whole game in local B2B
- Command (home screen) → work the pipeline: tap-to-call, tap-to-text,
  update statuses so the digest stays honest
- Hand out cards everywhere. The QR does the pitch for you.
