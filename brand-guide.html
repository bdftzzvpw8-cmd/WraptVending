01

FOUNDATION

Wrapt places, stocks, and services smart coolers that wear the host's brand. What we sell is an amenity: a modern market in three feet of wall space, at zero cost to the building.

The frame

Everything Wrapt makes lives inside clean, drawn structure. The box on the logo is the same box on the agreement, the sticker, and the site.

The blank canvas

Machines wear the client's wrap, not ours. Neutrality isn't a design taste — it's the product promise.

The quiet operator

Stocked, serviced, invisible. The brand behaves the way the service does: reliable, unshowy, always there.

Positioning statement

Wrapt turns unused wall space into a branded, self-serve market — placed free, stocked weekly, and dressed in whatever brand the building calls home.

Taglines

Primary: "Every machine is a canvas." · Secondary: "Your brand. Our shelf." · Service: "Stocked and serviced. You'll barely notice us."

VOICE

Wrapt speaks like a good landlord's favorite vendor: brief, certain, zero hype. The intelligence is in the machine, not the adjectives.

Sounds like

  • "Your brand. Our shelf."
  • "Stocked and serviced. You'll barely notice us."
  • "Three feet of wall. Zero cost. We'll handle the rest."

  • "AI-powered!", "revolutionary", "game-changing"

08

SYSTEM RULES

Non-negotiables

  1. The frame never opens. Four sides, square corners. It is the logo, the letterhead, the card border, the sticker.
  2. Only the word lives in the box. No descriptors, taglines, icons, or effects join WRAPT inside its frame — "Intelligence Vending" appears in running text and legal paperwork only.
  3. Charcoal or bone only for the logo. Never taupe, stone, or any client color — the neutrality is the strategy.
  4. Taupe stays under 5% of any surface. Labels and hairlines, nothing structural. Never body text.
  5. The machine never wears Wrapt. Client wrap on the machine; Wrapt lives on the 2-inch service sticker, the agreement, the polo, and the screen. The charcoal default wrap is for pre-client placements only.
  6. Caps in the frame, always. WRAPT is never written mixed-case inside its box.
  7. One 3px frame per surface. The logo's box is the only full frame in any layout — plates are 2px so the mark stays the loudest structure on the page.
  8. No new colors, faces, or effects. A layout that needs more than this system needs a better layout.
